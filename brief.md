# Brief: Columbus Roofing Company website

Source: `columbus-roofer-sitemap.csv` (24 pages). Company name is a placeholder: **"Your Roofing Company"** (domain yourroofingcompany.com). Do not invent facts, ratings, testimonials, licences, response times, or insurance promises. Use clearly marked placeholders.

## Goal
Make a Columbus, Ohio roofing company feel like a premium architectural craftsman, not a generic contractor. Primary conversion: **Request a Free Estimate** on every page.

## Audience
Columbus-area homeowners (Dublin, Hilliard, Worthington, Westerville, Gahanna, New Albany, Reynoldsburg, Grove City, Obetz, Groveport, Pickerington, Canal Winchester) with a leak, storm/hail damage, an ageing roof, or a planned replacement. They want trust, clarity, and low friction.

## Design direction ("Architectural Editorial")
- **Feel:** calm, confident, crafted. Like a high-end architecture studio or boutique hotel, applied to roofing.
- **Palette:** warm ivory/bone background (#F6F2EA), deep ink-slate (#14181F) for dark sections, one warm copper accent (#B8693A) for CTAs and key details, soft stone neutrals. No loud blues/reds. No gradients-on-white clichés.
- **Type:** large high-contrast serif display headlines (elegant, tight tracking) paired with a clean modern sans for body and UI; small uppercase tracked labels. Generous line height.
- **Space:** use space deliberately. 12-column grid, asymmetric layouts, big confident margins, oversized section numbers (01, 02...), thin hairline dividers. No empty dead zones and no cramped cards. Every viewport has one clear focal point.
- **Imagery:** large architectural roof photography (slate, shingle, standing-seam, dusk light), cropped tightly, with roofline/pitch line motifs as a graphic device.
- **Shape:** sharp or very slightly rounded corners (2-4px), pill-free, outlined secondary buttons, solid copper primary button.
- **Mobile:** every layout must collapse cleanly to a single column; sticky "Request a Free Estimate" button on mobile.

1. **Hero:** headline about protecting homes with craftsmanship, short subline, primary CTA "Request a Free Estimate", secondary "View Our Work", full-bleed architectural roof image.
2. **Trust strip:** four placeholder trust signals (licensed and insured, local to Columbus, workmanship warranty, free estimates), marked as to-be-verified.
3. **Services overview:** 5 services only (Roof Repair, Roof Replacement, Roof Installation, Roof Inspection, Storm and Hail Damage Repair), numbered editorial list, each linking to its page. **No Emergency Roofing.**
4. **Process:** inspect, plan, build, clean up, shown as a horizontal sequence.
5. **Featured projects:** 3 large project tiles (placeholder location, service, material).
6. **Customer reviews:** a review block with clearly placeholder reviews, no invented ratings.
7. **Service areas:** the 7 areas as a refined link grid with a stylised Columbus-area map.
8. **Final CTA band:** dark ink section, "Request a Free Estimate".
9. **Footer:** phone/email/address placeholders, service and location links, privacy policy link.

## Content rules (from sitemap)

- No extra service-area pages beyond the 7 listed.
- Every service and location page: Request a Free Estimate button + links to related pages.
